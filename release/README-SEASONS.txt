Seasons mod {VERSION} for {GAME} - the engine part of "The Sims: Seasons".

DO NOT INSTALL THIS ZIP ON YOUR OWN. IT IS NOT A COMPLETE MOD.
The files Seasons needs (objects, careers, clothes, art) and the installer are shipped in
https://github.com/SimReMods/Seasons - install Seasons from there. That installer downloads this zip itself.

What this part does: seasons and weather on normal lots (autumn leaves, snow on the ground, rain / snow / thunderstorm),
the season icon and calendar next to the funds, raincoats, umbrellas and winter clothes at the door, snow locations for
Seasons objects, the ExpansionPack8 objects folder and the extra careers of ExpansionPack8\SeasonsCareers.iff.

Contents:
  winmm.dll                 the mod loader (a proxy of the system winmm.dll)
  mods\{DLL}       the mod, for {GAME}
